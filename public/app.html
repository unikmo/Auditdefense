Executive case summary

Case C2024-8723 contains multiple review stages. A December 12, 2024 request included a 60-entry patient-record schedule. On March 13, 2026, Anthem stated that 59 of 60 claims were unsupported and demanded $64,733, consisting of $160 direct recovery for 97153 and $64,573 extrapolated recovery for 97155. The provider rebutted on March 20 and disputed the worksheet counts, document receipt, claim scope, rationale and extrapolation. A later 30-line review stage records 22 initially unsupported lines and 27 unsupported after rebuttal, alongside later reported NPI-related components totaling $567,096.77. The relationship between the two demand stages remains unresolved and is presented for attorney review without a legal conclusion.
