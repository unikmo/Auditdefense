FAQ

Built to organize the case, not replace counsel.

AuditDefend structures the factual and evidence workflow. Legal strategy, legal conclusions and advice remain with counsel.

Does the provider pay again if the attorney starts the case?

No. The product model is one fixed case fee based on the payer's asserted exposure, regardless of whether the provider or attorney originates the matter.

Is there an extra fee to find an attorney?

No for directory/self-selection, invitations and up to three initial participating-attorney introductions. Attorneys are independent professionals. A future optional human Match Concierge may carry a disclosed $195–$295 flat fee. Legal fees are contracted and paid directly between provider and attorney.

Does an invitation create representation or expose documents?

No. The sequence is Invitation sent → Conflict check pending → Engagement confirmed → Provider grants access. AuditDefend does not itself create an attorney-client relationship; engagement remains subject to the parties’ agreement and applicable professional rules.

Can I send the provider an intake link?

Yes. Create the matter, copy its structured intake link and send it through your usual client communication channel. Automated email delivery is not yet available.

Are counsel notes visible to the provider?

Counsel-only notes are separated from provider-facing summaries. Counsel chooses what is communicated to the provider.

Can I see the product before onboarding?

Yes. The sample workspace uses a redacted Anthem claim-review structure with no patient or member identifiers.
