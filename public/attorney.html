FAQ

Built to organize the case, not replace counsel.

AuditDefend structures the factual and evidence workflow. Legal strategy, legal conclusions and advice remain with counsel.

Does the provider pay again if the attorney starts the case?

No. The current product model is one case fee based on claim volume, regardless of whether the provider or attorney originates the matter.

Can I send the provider an intake link?

The provider-intake flow is implemented. Automated email delivery and live invitation acceptance remain external activation items in the pilot.

Are counsel notes visible to the provider?

The data model separates counsel-only notes from the provider-visible case root. Live Firestore rule deployment still requires verification before production reliance.

Can I see the product before onboarding?

Yes. The sample workspace uses the redacted Anthem claim-review structure supplied for this pilot.
