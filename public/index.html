THE PROBLEM

Audit response becomes expensive when the case has to be reconstructed from scratch.

Payer letters, spreadsheets, clinical documentation, enrollment records, rebuttal responses and provider explanations often arrive in different places. AuditDefend creates one claim-by-claim structure before the team spends hours figuring out what belongs to what.

1

Capture the audit

Payer, claim universe, exposure, deadlines, findings and current review stage.

2

Map what changed

Initial finding → evidence → rebuttal → payer response → final status, with independent issue categories.

3

Give counsel a case

Not a folder. Counsel gets issue chronology, evidence gaps, enrollment timing, exposure and the response record.

WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

The provider sees the simple story. Counsel keeps the forensic trail.

AuditDefend is not trying to make providers interpret payer coding language or make legal conclusions for attorneys. It organizes the factual record so both sides can work from the same case.
