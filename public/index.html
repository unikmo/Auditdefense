Exposure rule

The 73% initial error rate is based on claim-line count. It is not calculated from the dollar amount of the supported or unsupported claims. The case-wide asserted overpayment also cannot be derived simply by multiplying the sample error rate by total payments unless the payer's methodology expressly does so.

Attorney issue map

1. Payer authorityWhat exact rule, contract or program requirement is the payer relying on?
2. Claim universeWhich claims are affected by each allegation, and are any amounts double-counted?
3. Enrollment chronologyWas each billing/rendering NPI enrolled on the relevant DOS?
4. Documentation evidenceWhat was originally supplied, what was added on rebuttal, and what did the reviewer say remained missing?
5. Recovery mathSeparate sample paid amounts from the case-wide asserted overpayment.
6. Response recordPreserve the initial decision, rebuttal submission, final decision and source evidence.
