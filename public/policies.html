FOR PROVIDERS

What this means in plain English

A policy helps explain what the payer says was required. It does not prove that the rule applied to your exact claim or that the payer’s finding was correct.

  • Match the rule to the service date.
  • Confirm the plan, state and network.
  • Keep the exact version and source link.
  • Compare the rule with the record actually sent to the payer.
