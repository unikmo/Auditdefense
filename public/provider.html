THE 73% EXAMPLE

An “error rate” can hide what is really happening.

In the redacted case sample, 22 of 30 claim lines were initially unsupported, producing a 73% error rate by count. After rebuttal, 27 of 30 were unsupported. Five lines that initially passed later failed, including because of provider/NPI enrollment findings. AuditDefend keeps those changes visible.
