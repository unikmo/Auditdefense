PAYER AUDIT RESPONSE GUIDES

Start with the record—not assumptions about the outcome.

These guides explain how to organize a healthcare audit, overpayment demand and supporting evidence into a claim-by-claim workspace. They do not predict a court, payer or agency decision.

Use these guides to identify:
  • which claims and dates of service are actually in scope;
  • which finding applies to each claim;
  • where the supporting record is maintained;
  • which policy version and source the payer cites; and
  • which questions require attorney review.
