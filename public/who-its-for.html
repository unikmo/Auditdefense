WHO IT'S FOR

Who AuditDefend is for

Built for healthcare providers facing payer audits and the attorneys who defend them.

PROVIDERS

Healthcare providers who receive audits or overpayment demands

AuditDefend is designed for practices that must organize complex, multi-stage payer reviews — especially when documentation, enrollment timing, medical necessity, and changing findings all matter.

Best-fit specialties

ABA (Applied Behavior Analysis)

High documentation intensity: treatment plans, supervision, authorizations and enrollment. Useful when session notes, authorizations and enrollment records need to be mapped to each allegation. Read the ABA guide →

Outpatient physical, occupational and speech therapy

Time-based codes, functional progress documentation and multi-visit courses of care. Reviews can involve sampling and extrapolation across several years. Read the therapy guide →

Behavioral and mental health

Psychotherapy, intensive outpatient and substance-use programs. Common issues include time documentation, treatment plans, telehealth requirements and medical necessity. Read the behavioral-health guide →

Chiropractic

Documentation and medical-necessity review under Medicare and commercial payers.

Durable medical equipment (DME)

Coverage criteria, medical necessity and delivery documentation.

Pain management and interventional procedures

High-cost procedures and utilization patterns that can prompt focused reviews.

ATTORNEYS

Healthcare audit-defense counsel

Stop spending billable hours reconstructing the file.

Create the matter, send your provider client one guided intake link, and receive a structured case with claims, findings, rebuttal history, enrollment issues, evidence gaps, and exposure already organized.

You keep the forensic trail. The provider sees the plain-English status. Both sides work from the same workspace.

FIT

Not sure if it fits?

AuditDefend works best when the audit involves multiple claims, changing findings, documentation or enrollment issues, or potential extrapolation.

It is less necessary for simple single-claim reviews that can be handled with a short response letter.
